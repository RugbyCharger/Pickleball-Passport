## Phase 3: Idea Development (SCAMPER Method)

### SCAMPER #1: B2B Distribution - Refined Model

**"Passport Partners" - Points-Based Ambassador Network**

| SCAMPER | Selection | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Substitute | Free trip for director + spouse | Spouse becomes co-advocate, doubles word-of-mouth |
| Combine | LinkedIn outreach + Content marketing | Warm leads > cold leads |
| Adapt | Passport Points system (airline-style) | Gamification drives behavior |
| Modify | Amplify support, minimize cash upfront | High-touch, lower initial cost |
| Put to Other Uses | Market research from partners | Partners become eyes/ears in US |
| Eliminate | Cash commissions AND formal application | Simpler, points-based instead |
| Reverse | Directors recruit other directors | Peer credibility, viral growth |

**Refined Partner Program:**
- Directors earn Passport Points for every referral (not cash)
- Points redeem for: free trips, upgrades, spouse inclusion, exclusive experiences
- No formal application - self-service signup
- Dedicated partner success manager + turnkey marketing
- Directors recruit OTHER directors, earn points on their referrals
- Regular partner surveys for market intelligence
- Directors see transformation content BEFORE outreach

### SCAMPER #2: Transformation Product - Refined Model

**"Pickleball Passport Experience" - A Transformation Journey**

| SCAMPER | Selection | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Substitute | Thailand + Malaysia/Vietnam | Leverage exploding SEA pickleball scene |
| Combine | Medical + Celebration events | "Smile Reveal Party" creates shareable moment |
| Adapt | All-inclusive plus (cruise model) | Zero wallet moments, pure luxury |
| Modify | Amplify before/after, minimize logistics | Focus on outcome, handle everything |
| Put to Other Uses | Corporate wellness retreats | New B2B revenue stream |
| Eliminate | "Tour" language entirely | Position as "experience" or "journey" |
| Reverse | Trip continues virtually post-departure | Extended value, alumni community |

**Refined Product:**
- Multi-destination: Thailand flagship + Malaysia/Vietnam extension
- Positioning: "Experience" or "Journey" - NOT a tour
- All-inclusive plus: Everything handled, no decisions, no wallet
- Celebration events at transformation milestones
- Before/after narrative captured throughout
- Post-trip virtual community, coaching, reunion planning
- Corporate wellness packages for executive teams

### SCAMPER #3: Transformation Storytelling - Refined Model

**"Guest-Powered Transformation Stories"**

| SCAMPER | Selection | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Substitute | Guest-shot content with guided prompts | Authentic, UGC-ready |
| Combine | Before/after + Medical savings calculator | Emotion + rational justification |
| Adapt | Weight-loss industry before/after reveals | Proven format with stats |
| Modify | Amplify friendships, minimize sales language | Human connection sells |
| Put to Other Uses | Director recruitment content | Same content recruits partners AND customers |
| Eliminate | All company-created content | 100% guest-generated, radically authentic |
| Reverse | Show the fear beforehand | "I almost didn't go..." makes courage relatable |

**Refined Storytelling:**
- 100% guest-generated content with guided prompts
- Before/after reveals with stats (smile value, savings, skill improvement)
- Start with fear/hesitation: "I almost didn't go..."
- Focus on friendships formed, human connections
- Dual purpose: recruits guests AND director partners
- Zero sales language - let stories speak
