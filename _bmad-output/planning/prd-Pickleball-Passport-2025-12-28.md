# Product Requirements Document: Pickleball Passport

## Executive Summary

Pickleball Passport is a luxury transformation travel platform that combines world-class pickleball, medical tourism, and wellness experiences in Thailand. This PRD defines the digital product ecosystem required to deliver "life upgrades disguised as vacations" to affluent US retirees (55+) through an integrated multi-platform solution.

**Product Vision:** Create the world's first transformation tourism platform where guests forge genuine friendships while investing in themselves through pickleball, medical care, and wellness—all supported by seamless digital experiences that feel as premium as the physical journey.

**Core Digital Products:**
1. **Marketing Website** - Luxury-tier discovery and application experience
2. **Booking Platform** - Package configurator and reservation system
3. **Payment System** - Multi-installment, high-value transaction processing
4. **Mobile App** - Pre-trip, during-trip, and alumni experience
5. **Partner Portal** - B2B2C distribution platform for club directors

---

## Product Strategy

### Market Opportunity

**Target Market Size:**
- Thailand medical tourism: USD $2.57B-$31.5B (2024) growing 10.49%-18.93% CAGR
- Global pickleball market: USD $1.61B (2024) growing to $3.52B by 2032
- Asia-Pacific pickleball: 24.5% annual growth rate
- US affluent retirees 55+: 150K+ at The Villages alone

**Competitive Gap:**
- **ZERO** direct competitors combining pickleball + medical tourism + wellness in Thailand
- **ZERO** pickleball tour operators focused on Thailand/Southeast Asia
- All existing pickleball tours (Ben Johns, Pickleball Getaways) focus on Caribbean/Central America
- 2-3 year first-mover window before market saturation

**Unique Value Proposition:**
- Medical cost savings ($10K-15K) essentially fund luxury vacation
- Transformation outcomes (new smile, renewed body, lifelong friendships) vs transactional "fun trips"
- On-the-ground Thailand pickleball network (impossible for competitors to replicate)
- B2B2C distribution via club directors (untapped channel)

### Product Positioning

**Positioning Statement:**
"For affluent US retirees who play pickleball and seek meaningful transformation, Pickleball Passport is the luxury travel experience that delivers life-changing journeys where guests forge genuine friendships while investing in themselves—unlike existing pickleball tours that offer transactional fun weeks, we create lasting personal transformation through world-class medical care, wellness, and authentic cultural immersion in Thailand."

**Brand Essence:**
- NOT: "Pickleball tour with medical add-ons"
- NOT: "Medical tourism with pickleball"
- YES: "Life upgrade disguised as vacation"
- YES: "Where pickleball meets world-class wellness and medical care"

**Tagline Options:**
- "Come for pickleball. Leave transformed."
- "Your next chapter starts with a paddle."
- "Where transformation meets luxury."

### Success Criteria

**Phase 1 (Year 1): Prove the Model**
- Execute 1 flawless trip with 8-12 guests
- Achieve 100% guest satisfaction (NPS 70+)
- Capture 3-5 video testimonials
- Zero medical complications
- Positive ROI on first trip
- Establish 5-10 director partnerships

**Phase 2 (Year 2): Scale Through Distribution**
- 40-60 guests across 4-6 trips
- 20-30 active director partnerships generating referrals
- 50%+ bookings from word-of-mouth/referrals
- Profitability on every trip

**Digital Product Metrics:**
- Website conversion: 15-20% (application submission)
- Application-to-booking: 40-50%
- Mobile app adoption: 90%+ of guests
- Partner portal usage: 80%+ of active directors
- Alumni community engagement: 60%+ monthly active

   - Managed through third-party integration

- PCI-DSS compliant (Stripe handles)
- SSL encryption
- Fraud detection (Stripe Radar)

- Payment success → Immediate confirmation email
- Booking summary PDF attached
- Calendar invite (.ics file) for departure date
- Next steps: "Access your member portal"
- Assign dedicated concierge contact

**Failed Payment Handling:**
- Retry logic (3 attempts over 7 days)
- Email + SMS notification
- Phone call from concierge for high-value bookings
- Cancellation notice at day 10 if unresolved

**Success Metrics:**
- Payment completion rate: >95%
- Installment plan usage: 40-50%
- Payment failure rate: <5%

The mobile app serves as the primary interface for guests during their transformation journey, providing itinerary management, concierge access, social connection, and post-trip alumni community engagement.

### Key Objectives

1. Replace paper itineraries with dynamic, real-time trip management
2. Provide instant concierge access (24/7 support)
3. Facilitate social connections among guests
4. Capture memories and transformation stories
5. Drive repeat bookings and referrals through alumni features

### Platform Strategy

**Phase 1 (MVP):** iOS + Android via React Native (Expo)

**Phase 2 (Enhanced):** Native features
- Biometric authentication
- Apple Pay / Google Pay

### Security Architecture

**Authentication:**
- JWT tokens (short-lived: 15 min access, 7-day refresh)
- Biometric (mobile app)
- MFA optional for high-value accounts

**Authorization:**
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
  - Guest: Own bookings, profile, trip data
  - Partner: Own referrals, portal features
  - Admin: All data, operations dashboard
- Row-level security (PostgreSQL RLS)

**Data Protection:**
- Encryption at rest (database, S3)
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- PII redaction in logs
- GDPR compliance (data export, deletion requests)

**Payment Security:**
- PCI-DSS Level 1 (Stripe)
- No card data stored (tokenization)
- 3D Secure authentication
- Fraud detection (Stripe Radar)

### Scalability Strategy

**Phase 1 (Year 1): 8-12 guests, 1 trip**
- Serverless functions (auto-scaling)
- Managed database (Supabase: up to 10K rows)
- CDN caching (Vercel Edge)
- Cost: ~$200-500/month

**Phase 2 (Year 2): 40-60 guests, 4-6 trips**

**Phase 3 (Year 3+): 100+ guests, 10+ trips**
- Microservices (if needed, likely not)
- Multi-region deployment (global guests)

- Cost: ~$3K-5K/month

## User Experience (UX) Guidelines

### Design Principles

1. **Luxury First:** Every interaction should feel premium (Four Seasons standard)
2. **Trust & Transparency:** Clear pricing, credentials, policies (address "Is this legitimate?")
3. **Simplicity for 55+:** Not tech-savvy friendly, large text, minimal steps
4. **Emotional Storytelling:** Transformation outcomes, not features
5. **White-Glove Service:** Human support always accessible (chat, phone, video)

### Visual Design System

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep Ocean Blue (#003D5C) - trust, luxury
- Secondary: Warm Gold (#D4AF37) - premium, transformation
- Neutral: Crisp White (#FFFFFF), Soft Gray (#F5F5F5)
- Accent: Coral Sunset (#FF6B6B) - CTAs, energy

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Serif (Playfair Display, Cormorant) - elegance
- Body: Sans-serif (Inter, Open Sans) - readability
- Size: Minimum 16px (mobile), 18px (desktop) for 55+ readability

**Imagery:**
- Professional lifestyle photography (no stock photos)
- Authentic guest photos (with consent)
- Cinematic videos (Thailand landscapes, pickleball action, cultural moments)
- Before/after transformations (tasteful, not clinical)

**Spacing & Layout:**
- Generous white space (luxury aesthetic)
- Maximum 2-column layout (avoid clutter)
- Sticky CTAs (always accessible)

### Accessibility Standards

**WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance:**
- Color contrast ratio: 4.5:1 (text), 3:1 (UI elements)
- Keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader optimization (semantic HTML, ARIA labels)
- Alt text for all images
- Captions for videos

**55+ User Accommodations:**
- Large touch targets (minimum 44x44px)
- Clear labels (no icons without text)
- Avoid jargon ("Apply to Join" is clearer than "Submit Inquiry")
- Confirm destructive actions (double-check before delete/cancel)
