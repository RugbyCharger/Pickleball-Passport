## 1. Company Overview

The Pickleball Passport is a US-based travel company offering curated 9-day pickleball and cultural travel experiences through Thailand. Our guests are primarily American adults (ages 35–65), mix of couples and solo travelers, who play pickleball recreationally or competitively. Groups are capped at 16 guests per departure, with a dedicated trip host traveling with each group.

We currently operate two routes, both 9 days / 8 nights:

| Route | Cities | Hotels | Season |
|-------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Bangkok + Hua Hin** | Bangkok → Hua Hin → Bangkok | The Peninsula Bangkok + Anantara Hua Hin Resort | Year-round |
| **Bangkok + Chiang Mai** | Bangkok → Chiang Mai → Bangkok | The Peninsula Bangkok + Anantara Chiang Mai Resort | May – January |

Multiple departures per year. See Section 6 for upcoming dates.

We will have our own trip host traveling with each group. However, we are also interested in having a **local Thai host** provided by the DMC to accompany the group throughout the trip.

We'd like to see pricing and profiles for this role. Ideal qualifications:

- **Fluent in English and Thai** — able to translate, negotiate, and handle any local situations
- **Cultural ambassador** — can provide context on Thai culture, customs, etiquette, and history beyond what activity guides cover
- **Logistics coordinator** — on-the-ground point person for the DMC, handles restaurant confirmations, transport timing, vendor communication in real-time
- **Personable and guest-facing** — this person is part of the trip experience, not just behind the scenes. Should be comfortable socializing with a group of 16 American adults
- **Available for the full 9-day trip** — travels with the group from Day 1 arrival to Day 9 departure
- **WhatsApp-reachable** by both our trip host and your ops team at all times
